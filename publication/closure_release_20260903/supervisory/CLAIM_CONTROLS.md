# Claim controls

## Allowed before G1

- The A5 G0 return reconstructs the candidate on two physically distinct host-evidence legs.
- Both host JUnit records contain 402 full and 169 targeted passing tests.
- No A5 scientific execution occurred in the submitted return.
- One G1-only receipt has been issued.

## Allowed after a successful G1

- The deterministic small-system limiting-case implementation passes the frozen G1 consistency and sanity gate.

## Allowed after M02 only if passed

- The transport-calibrated stochastic surrogate has a restricted, quantified domain in which selected squeezing observables agree with the constrained exact/Krylov comparator.

## Allowed after a stable negative M02 boundary

- The stochastic surrogate has a quantified failure boundary under the tested models, systems, time windows, and metrics.

## Forbidden without new evidence

- Exact lithium mobile-hole dynamics.
- A universal mobile-hole obstruction.
- A no-go theorem.
- Robust 3D squeezing recovery.
- Constructive 3 dB recovery.
- Cross-dimensional predictive validity.
- PRL/PRX Quantum readiness.
- Public reusable-software readiness.
