# Claim boundary for the first draft

## Supported language

- “In a selected 3x3x3 stochastic moving-occupancy surrogate, the preregistered
  static-only minus mobile-plus-spin-density contrast was negative in both the
  primary and frozen confirmation blocks.”
- “The primary mean was -0.411797 dB (hierarchical SE 0.048709 dB; 95% interval
  [-0.517604, -0.307798] dB).”
- “The confirmation mean was -0.474424 dB (hierarchical SE 0.039405 dB; 95%
  interval [-0.571780, -0.376832] dB).”
- “The primary and confirmation effects met the preregistered ±0.25 dB
  equivalence criterion.”
- “The frozen A5 software/custody object passed G0 on two physically distinct
  hosts, with 402 full and 169 targeted tests passing on each host.”
- “The single authorised G1 attempt terminated without accepted scientific
  output; the preserved A5 diagnostics do not identify the child-level cause.”

## Forbidden or unsupported language

- exact lithium mobile-hole dynamics;
- a causal mobile-hole mechanism;
- a universal obstruction or no-go theorem;
- constructive or 3 dB recovery;
- cross-dimensional or broad finite-size generalisation;
- a scientific G1 null/failure conclusion;
- successful M01/M02 validation;
- PRL/PRX Quantum readiness or public reusable-software readiness.

## Drafting rule

Every physics claim must say “selected stochastic moving-occupancy surrogate”
or an equally restrictive formulation. Treat G1 as a disclosed terminal
execution event, not a scientific result. Separate reproducibility/custody
evidence from scientific validation.
